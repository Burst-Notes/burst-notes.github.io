Capture Thoughts in Quick Bursts

Burst Notes is a lightweight macOS utility that lets you capture ideas directly into your existing Markdown files from any application. It runs silently in the background—no dock icon, no visible windows—and activates instantly via a global keyboard shortcut.

Hit the shortcut, type to filter your document's headers, select a section, add your note, and press Return. The interaction completes in under ten seconds, vanishing without disrupting your workflow.

Seamless Global Capture

Trigger notes from anywhere with a customizable keyboard shortcut. No app switching, no context loss. Your thoughts land precisely where they belong in your Markdown files.

Header-Based Navigation

Filter and select sections by typing the first characters of headers. Arrow keys refine your choice. Frequent sections rise to the top based on your patterns, making repeated access effortless.

Dual-Mode Editing

Edit in real-time Visual mode or switch to Source with a live preview pane. Native GitHub-flavored checkboxes handle tasks seamlessly—all keyboard-driven, zero mouse required.

Zero Overhead Design

Works with standard .md files—no databases, no proprietary formats. Idle resource usage is negligible, with no telemetry or network activity. Built in Swift and AppKit for macOS Ventura (13.0+) on Intel and Apple Silicon.

How It Works

1. Press Global Shortcut

From any app, hit your shortcut. The capture window appears instantly.

2. Filter and Select

Type to filter headers in your Markdown file. Use arrows to choose the section.

3. Type and Submit

Add your note in Visual or Source mode. Press Return to save and vanish.

4. Resume Seamlessly

Back to your work in seconds. Notes integrate directly into your files.
